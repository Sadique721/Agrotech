Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

At AgroTech Innovations, accessible from agrotech-wqj3.onrender.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by AgroTech and how we use it.

Information We Collect

If you register for an account or contact us, we may collect personal information including but not limited to:

  • Account Info: Username, Email address, and Password hashes.
  • Profile Info: Full Name, Phone Number, State, District, Farm Size, Cultivated Crops, Farming Experience, Bio, and Profile Pictures.
  • Inquiries: Contact form details (Name, Email, Message) and newsletter signup emails.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our agricultural platform.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our weather forecasting and crop advisory tools.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our platform to optimize farm-support services.
  • Communicate with you regarding service callback requests and system notifications.
  • Prevent fraud, secure user accounts, and enforce safety policies.

Data Security & Retention

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal details. Media uploads are scanned to ensure security, and user passwords are securely hashed. We store your data on secure database systems and retain it only as long as necessary to serve your account or inquiries.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@agrotech.com.
