Who We Are & What Drives Us

AgroTech is dedicated to modernizing farming practices across India with smart technological solutions.

🎯 Our Mission

To equip every farmer with affordable, real-time data tools—from automated smart irrigation and live weather tracking to drone surveillance—ensuring maximum crop yields while preserving natural resources.

🚀 Our Vision

To build a climate-resilient, data-driven agricultural ecosystem where technology bridges the gap between traditional farming wisdom and modern sustainable food security.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does AgroTech calculate real-time weather advisories?

AgroTech links real-time Open-Meteo satellite feed with humidity, temperature, and precipitation sensors to generate dynamic recommendations for irrigation, pesticide spraying, and crop harvesting.

Can small-scale farmers afford AgroTech IoT devices?

Yes! We offer affordable pay-per-use drone spraying, community sensor shares, and free mobile weather advisories tailored specifically for smallholder farms.

Which Indian states does AgroTech currently cover?

We provide full real-time weather monitoring and agricultural services across all 28 states and 8 Union Territories in India including Gujarat, Maharashtra, Punjab, UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, and West Bengal.
